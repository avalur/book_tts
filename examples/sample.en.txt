Chapter 1. A quiet beginning

The library opened early. Anna found a small table by the window and placed her book beside a cup of tea. Outside, the street was waking up. Inside, there was time to read one page carefully.

“Shall we begin?” she asked.

“At the beginning,” said Daniel. “And slowly enough to enjoy it.”

Chapter 2. The next page

A good audiobook should leave room for the story. The voice stays clear, the pauses feel natural, and every chapter arrives in the right order. Tomorrow there would be another page, another walk, and a little more time to listen.
